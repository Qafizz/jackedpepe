FAQs

When does the presale open? When its legal terms are final. The date, eligibility and limits will be posted here first.

Who can buy? Accredited investors, friends / family / business associates under the legal exemptions, and eligible investors through a registered exempt market dealer or funding portal, with yearly limits for retail buyers. Outside Canada: non-US persons, or US accredited investors after verification. Sanctioned countries and mainland China are blocked. Everyone passes an identity check (KYC) first.

Can I get a refund? If the sale's minimum is not reached it can be cancelled and refunded, and buyers have a 2-day cooling-off right. Refunds go back in the same asset and path, minus network fees.

What do I sign? An offering memorandum, subscription agreement and risk acknowledgement, prepared by legal counsel, before any payment can be made.

Is there a contract address? Not yet. Anything posted elsewhere is a scam.
